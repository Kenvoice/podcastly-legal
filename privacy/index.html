Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

This policy explains what data Podcastly ("the app", "we") collects, why, and who it is shared with. Podcastly is operated by Kenzo Marian Weiss, a private individual based in Germany (full contact details in the Imprint).

1. Data we collect

DataPurposeSource
Email address, password (hashed)Account creation and sign-inYou, via Supabase Auth
Saved words, review schedule, listening progress, study streakSync your library and flashcards across devicesGenerated as you use the app, stored via Supabase
Podcast episode audioGenerating a transcript for an episode nobody has transcribed yetSent to AssemblyAI (transcription provider); not sent again once a transcript is cached
Short text snippets (words, example sentences)Generating natural-sounding pronunciation audioSent to Google Cloud Text-to-Speech
Your questions and the dictionary lookups you ask aboutThe in-app AI chat and definition featuresSent to DeepSeek (AI provider)
Subscription/purchase statusUnlocking Pro featuresApple's App Store and RevenueCat; we never see your payment details

Saved words and your review schedule are also kept locally on your device (in an on-device database) so the app works offline; this local copy never leaves your device except via the sync described above.

2. Legal basis

We process this data under Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (necessary to provide the service you signed up for). We do not run advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell data.

3. Third-party processors

  • Supabase — account authentication and database hosting.
  • AssemblyAI — speech-to-text transcription of podcast audio.
  • Google Cloud (Text-to-Speech) — generating spoken audio for words and sentences.
  • DeepSeek — AI-generated answers and dictionary definitions.
  • Apple (App Store) and RevenueCat — processing purchases and verifying subscription status.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States and, in the case of DeepSeek, in China. Where a provider is located outside the EEA, we rely on the safeguards that provider offers (such as standard contractual clauses) to the extent they are available; we recommend not submitting sensitive personal information in chat questions or search terms.

4. Retention

Your account data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time (see Section 6). Cached podcast transcripts are shared, anonymous infrastructure — they are not tied to any individual account and are kept so the same episode does not need to be transcribed twice.

5. Children

Podcastly is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children under that age.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data, and to object to or restrict its processing. To exercise any of these rights, or to request removal of a podcast feed you hold rights to, contact us at kenzo.voicebox@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority; for the controller named above, that is the Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Rheinland-Pfalz.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
